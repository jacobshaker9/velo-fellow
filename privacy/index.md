# Velo Fellow Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026
**Developer:** Jacob Shaker
**Contact:** jacobshaker9@gmail.com

Velo Fellow is a running and cycling companion for Apple Watch and iPhone. During a Run or Ride it gives heart-rate-zone feedback and a subtle haptic alert when you stay outside your target heart-rate zone. Velo Fellow is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Health data (Apple HealthKit)

With your permission, Velo Fellow uses Apple HealthKit on your Apple Watch to:

- **read your heart rate** during a running or cycling workout, to work out your current heart-rate zone and decide when to alert you;
- **read and save workout distance** (walking + running distance for runs, cycling distance for rides). This is optional; if you don't allow it, workouts have no distance;
- **save your runs and rides as workouts** in the Apple Health app;
- on watchOS 27 and later, **read your preferred heart-rate zones** from Apple Health, so Velo Fellow can use the same zones as the Health app.

You can change or withdraw these permissions at any time in Settings → Health (or in the Health app). Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and it is never sold or shared with third parties.

## What Velo Fellow stores, and where

- **Settings** (target heart-rate zone, alert delay, and, if you enter them, your maximum heart rate, resting heart rate and age, which are used only to calculate heart-rate zones) are stored on your Apple Watch and iPhone.
- **Workout summaries** (activity type, run or ride; date; elapsed, active and paused time; distance; average heart rate; target heart-rate zone and the zone boundaries used; time in each zone; number of zone exits; number and times of alerts) are created on your Apple Watch and stored on your iPhone.
- **During a workout**, your Apple Watch sends a small live status (activity, current heart-rate zone, elapsed time, distance, pace or speed) to your own iPhone so it can show a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Anyone who can see your iPhone's Lock Screen can see this information. You can turn Live Activities off in Settings → Velo Fellow.
- All of this moves only between **your own** Apple Watch and iPhone, using Apple's WatchConnectivity. Velo Fellow has **no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs**. The developer does not receive your health data, settings, or workout history.
- Deleting the app deletes its stored settings and workout history from that device. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there.

## Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you take part in the TestFlight beta, Apple provides the developer with information you choose to send, such as feedback and screenshots, and with crash reports and basic device and usage information, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms and privacy policy. This is handled by Apple, not by Velo Fellow. If you email feedback, your email and message are used only to respond and improve the app.

## Children

Velo Fellow is not directed at children under 13.
